Disaster Preparedness & Safety Protocol

Actionable guidelines before, during, and after flood events

BEFORE A FLOOD
  • Prepare 72-hour Emergency Go-Bag.
  • Store clean drinking water (1 gal/person/day).
  • Secure important documents in waterproof pouches.
  • Move electrical appliances to high ground.
DURING A FLOOD
  • Do NOT walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Turn off main electrical breaker if water rises.
  • Obey local LGU forced evacuation commands.
  • Avoid touching submerged electrical lines.
AFTER A FLOOD
  • Wait for official clearance before returning home.
  • Disinfect submerged areas to prevent disease.
  • Wear rubber boots to avoid Leptospirosis.
  • Inspect electrical wiring with a professional.

Platform Mission & Overview

ABOUT BSAFE

BSAFE (Bulacan Safety and Flood Emergency) is a centralized disaster monitoring web portal designed to deliver high-resolution, accessible, and actionable flood telemetry for the Province of Bulacan. Modeled after modern disaster assessment standards, BSAFE equips citizens, local emergency responders, and community leaders with essential tools to mitigate flood hazards and execute timely emergency responses.

Vulnerability Context of Bulacan

Due to low-lying topography, coastal proximity along Manila Bay, riverine spillovers from Angat and Pampanga dams, and astronomical high tides, Bulacan requires continuous hazard monitoring and multi-channel public dissemination.
