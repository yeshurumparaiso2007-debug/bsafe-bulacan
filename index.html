Disaster Preparedness & Safety Protocol

Actionable guidelines before, during, and after flood events

BEFORE A FLOOD
  • Prepare 72-hour Emergency Go-Bag.
  • Store clean drinking water (1 gal/person/day).
  • Secure important documents in waterproof pouches.
  • Move electrical appliances to high ground.
DURING A FLOOD
  • Do NOT walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Turn off main electrical breaker if water rises.
  • Obey local LGU forced evacuation commands.
  • Avoid touching submerged electrical lines.
AFTER A FLOOD
  • Wait for official clearance before returning home.
  • Disinfect submerged areas to prevent disease.
  • Wear rubber boots to avoid Leptospirosis.
  • Inspect electrical wiring with a professional.

Platform Overview & Mission

ABOUT US

BSAFE (Bulacan Safety and Flood Emergency) is an informative web portal inspired by Project NOAH (Nationwide Operational Assessment of Hazards) and is created to provide accessible and easy to understand information about flooding in selected areas of Bulacan. This website's purpose is to help residents to be aware of flood risks and to better prepare them for possible flood related emergencies.

Vulnerability Context of Bulacan

The Province of Bulacan is vulnerable to flooding because of its low-lying areas, proximity to rivers and other bodies of water, heavy rainfall, high tides, and possible dam water releases. Because of these risks, BSAFE focuses on providing useful information about flood conditions, warnings, safety measures, and emergency preparedness.

Our Student Development Team

We are a team of students who worked together to conceptualize, research, design, and create the web portal BSAFE. Through our combined efforts, we aimed to create a simple and accessible website that provides useful flood-related information for selected communities in Bulacan. We hope that BSAFE can help promote greater awareness and preparedness for flooding among individuals and communities.
